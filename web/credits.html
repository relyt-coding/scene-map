Where this comes from

Scene Map is a reader for an open dataset. Nothing here is scraped, guessed or generated.

Locations and titles

The places, the films, shows, anime, manga and games, and the links between them come from Movie Scene Map, published under CC0. That project reads Wikipedia in English and twenty-six other languages, plus Wikidata.

Because some of those columns are read from Wikipedia, credit Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) for them. The dataset itself makes no claim on top of the CC0 offer.

Filmed at vs set in

Films and TV series are placed where they were shot. Video games, anime and manga were filmed nowhere, so they are placed where their story is set. The two are always labelled separately here. These are not the same claim, and a place is not a scene.

Photographs

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, each under its own licence. Every place card names the photographer and licence, and links to it.

Basemap

Map tiles from OpenFreeMap, built from OpenStreetMap data.

Routes ("view in order")

The dataset has no episode or scene order, so ordered routes are written by hand for one title at a time. The JoJo route is built from the manga and anime, with the JoJo Wiki location reference gallery as a cross-check. Scene labels are editorial, not data.

What this is not

Not affiliated with Movie Scene Map, Wikipedia, Wikimedia or any studio. Coverage is only as good as the open data: some titles have no place recorded, and some only a country.
